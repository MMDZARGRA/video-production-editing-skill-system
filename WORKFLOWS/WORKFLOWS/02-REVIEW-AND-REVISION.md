# Workflow: Review and Revision

1. Send the review link with the brief, version purpose, deadline, and requested feedback scope.
2. Ask reviewers to provide timecode, observed viewer problem, desired outcome, and priority.
3. Consolidate conflicting notes; the designated approver resolves strategic conflicts.
4. Triage P0 (release blockers), P1 (brief-impacting), P2 (polish). Estimate cost only after meaning is clear.
5. Log accept/decline/clarify decisions with rationale, revise, then perform a fresh QC pass.
6. Never silently incorporate a request that changes scope, factual claims, or platform strategy—return to the brief owner.
