# Workflow: Platform Adaptation

1. Start from the approved master and restate the fixed promise, proof, CTA, and brand/legal requirements.
2. For each platform, define viewer context, aspect ratio, safe area, expected opening speed, target duration, caption behavior, packaging, and metric.
3. Recut the opening for native context; do not simply crop a horizontal master when the visual priority changes.
4. Reframe/replace graphics and B-roll so the main subject and text remain readable on mobile.
5. Export, QC, and name each version; verify title, thumbnail, captions, links, and CTA in the published context.
6. Compare performance only where audience, goal, and distribution conditions are comparable.
