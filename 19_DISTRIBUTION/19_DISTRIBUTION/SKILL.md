# Distribution

**Purpose:** place the right creative package in the right context at the right time. Distribution starts with audience behavior and platform intent, not a last-minute upload.

**Procedure:** select channel and format; adapt opening/length/safe area; craft title, thumbnail, caption, description, CTA, and publishing sequence; plan community response and repurposing.

**Practice:** turn one master into a channel plan for three platforms, explaining what remains fixed and what changes. Quality means every adaptation preserves the promise while respecting its viewing context.
