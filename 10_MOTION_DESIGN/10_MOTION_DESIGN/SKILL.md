# Motion Design

**Purpose:** make hierarchy, change, and brand feel visible over time. Motion must improve comprehension or attention—not decorate a weak message.

**Procedure:** define hierarchy and timing; establish type/grid/color rules; animate with anticipation, clear change, and readable settling; test at actual mobile viewing size.

**Practice:** create a five-screen title package with one reusable lower third and one data callout. Quality means motion reinforces reading order, remains legible, and can be updated without rebuilding.
