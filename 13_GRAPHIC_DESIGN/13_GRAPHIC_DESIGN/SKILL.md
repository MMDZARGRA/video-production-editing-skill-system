# Graphic Design

**Purpose:** make information and brand clear through hierarchy, layout, contrast, space, color, and image choice. Design for the viewing size and duration, not the artboard.

**Procedure:** establish grid and focal point; rank information; use contrast and whitespace; test safe areas; keep a limited consistent visual vocabulary.

**Practice:** design a thumbnail, end card, and 9:16 information card from one brief. Quality means a viewer understands the main message in a glance and the pieces clearly belong together.
