# Scriptwriting

**Purpose:** create a spoken-and-seen path to one promised outcome. Write for ear, eye, and edit: short clauses, concrete language, visual opportunities, and intentional breathing room.

**Structure:** hook → context/problem → escalating value → proof/example → resolution → CTA. Read aloud, remove throat-clearing, and mark what must be seen rather than said.

**Practice:** write, table-read, and cut a 150-word script to 110 words while retaining its promise. Quality means the opening makes a credible promise and every line advances story, trust, or action.
