# Editing

**Purpose:** shape meaning, pace, emotion, and clarity from recorded material. Edit decisions follow viewer attention, not merely beat matching or visual novelty.

**Procedure:** organize/select; make assembly; solve story in rough cut; solve performance/rhythm in fine cut; use J/L cuts, B-roll, silence, and transitions intentionally; preserve versions.

**Practice:** create three 45-second cuts from the same footage for calm, urgent, and educational intent. Quality means every cut either changes information, emotion, attention, or time.
