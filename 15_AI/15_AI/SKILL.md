# AI

**Purpose:** accelerate low-risk tasks—ideation, transcription, logging, rough selects, caption drafting, cleanup—while retaining human ownership of truth, taste, rights, and final approval.

**Rules:** disclose and label generated material where required; verify claims and outputs; never upload confidential media without authorization; record tool, prompt/input, version, and rights terms when material affects final output.

**Practice:** use an AI transcript to make selects, compare it to source media, correct errors, and document the time saved and failure modes. Quality means human review catches meaningful errors before they reach an audience.
