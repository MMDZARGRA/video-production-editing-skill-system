# Color

**Purpose:** correct technical inconsistency first, then create a controlled look. Work in a known color-managed pipeline and protect skin tone, highlight detail, and deliverable standards.

**Procedure:** normalize input; balance exposure and white balance; match shots; qualify only when needed; apply look; verify scopes and exports on a calibrated/reliable display.

**Practice:** match six shots from two lighting conditions, then create two distinct but natural looks. Quality means cuts do not distract through color shifts and the look supports story rather than hiding errors.
