# Analytics

**Purpose:** learn whether packaging, opening, value, and CTA performed for the intended audience. Read metrics in context and convert them into a single testable next decision.

**Procedure:** establish objective and baseline; review reach, CTR, early retention, average watch time, completion, engagement, and conversion; identify one drop-off or strength; form a hypothesis; change one variable next time.

**Practice:** compare two similar posts and write a hypothesis that distinguishes hook, audience, packaging, and distribution timing. Quality means the conclusion is evidence-based and leads to a controlled experiment—not a superstition.
