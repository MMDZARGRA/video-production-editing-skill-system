# Directing

**Purpose:** align performance, shot coverage, sound, art direction, and pace around one intention. Direct actions and objectives, not vague emotions.

**Procedure:** communicate the scene's viewer effect; give talent playable direction; confirm blocking and eyeline; collect a safe take and alternate; check coverage against the edit plan.

**Practice:** direct a two-person 30-second scene in three tonal versions using the same script. Quality means collaborators can repeat the intended result and the editor has meaningful options.
