# Cinematography

**Purpose:** use framing, lens, camera position, movement, exposure, and focus to direct attention and feeling. Technical correctness supports, but does not replace, visual intention.

**Procedure:** choose shot purpose; control exposure and white balance; protect focus; establish wide/medium/close coverage; use movement only when it adds information or energy.

**Practice:** tell one silent 20-second action with five deliberate shots. Quality means the viewer can follow action and priority without dialogue, and images match across the sequence.
