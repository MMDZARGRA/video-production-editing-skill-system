# Research

**Purpose:** provide accurate material and useful insight for a specific audience. Separate facts, interpretations, references, and assumptions; retain source, date, and rights context.

**Procedure:** define questions; find primary or credible sources; verify important claims against a second source; extract audience language; turn findings into a tension, insight, and proof inventory.

**Practice:** make a research pack for a 60-second explainer with five verified claims, three audience objections, and one insight. Quality means every claim can be traced and every source changes a creative decision.
