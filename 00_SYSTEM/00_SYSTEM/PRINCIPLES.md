# Operating Principles

1. **Outcome before technique.** Every cut, shot, graphic, and sound must support a viewer outcome: attention, understanding, feeling, trust, or action.
2. **Story before polish.** Lock the message and structure before spending time on animation, grading, or sound sweetening.
3. **One source of truth.** The brief, version log, feedback log, and delivery spec live with the project.
4. **Separate decisions from execution.** Decide audience, promise, format, and review criteria before opening the timeline.
5. **Protect the original.** Use a clear ingest process, backups, proxies, and non-destructive edits.
6. **Review against criteria, not taste alone.** Ask whether the video meets the brief, holds attention, is accurate, intelligible, and technically clean.
7. **Make feedback actionable.** Feedback names a timecode, observed issue, desired change, and priority.
8. **Measure the promise.** Analytics are evidence about the opening, value delivery, and call to action—not a verdict on personal worth.
9. **Build reusable assets carefully.** Templates speed repeated work; they should not make every project look identical.
10. **Close the loop.** Each published project must create one documented learning and one next experiment.

## Golden rules

- Never delete or overwrite camera originals.
- Never start a fine cut without an approved rough-cut direction.
- Never export without a delivery specification and QC pass.
- Never publish a claim, quote, statistic, music track, image, or logo whose rights and accuracy are unknown.
- Never accept vague feedback as a task; translate it into a decision.
