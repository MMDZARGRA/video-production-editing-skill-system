# Post-production QC Checklist

- [ ] Story matches the approved brief; hook, value, proof, CTA, and logo/brand use are intentional.
- [ ] No flash frames, jumpy image, black frame, missing media, typo, wrong subtitle, or placeholder remains.
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible; music/effects do not mask it; cuts avoid clicks and abrupt ambience changes.
- [ ] Exposure, white balance, skin tone, and shot matching are consistent; graphics have readable contrast.
- [ ] Captions are accurate, timed, safe-area compliant, and correctly encoded/delivered.
- [ ] Export resolution, frame rate, codec, audio channels, duration, filename, and metadata match the manifest.
- [ ] Watch the final export end-to-end on a second device before release.
