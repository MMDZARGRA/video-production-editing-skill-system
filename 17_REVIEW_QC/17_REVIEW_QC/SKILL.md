# Review and Quality Control

**Purpose:** turn approval into a controlled decision and protect audiences from factual, legal, editorial, accessibility, and technical mistakes.

**Procedure:** set review goal and approver; collect timecoded notes; triage by risk; resolve one decision at a time; run fresh end-to-end QC on the exported file; log approval and exceptions.

**Practice:** QC a one-minute video with intentional defects, then write actionable notes and an acceptance recommendation. Quality means blockers are found before release and taste feedback does not obscure the brief.
