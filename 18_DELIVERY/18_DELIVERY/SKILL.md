# Delivery

**Purpose:** supply correct, traceable masters and versions that play as intended wherever they are received. Delivery includes metadata, captions, thumbnails, rights records, and archive—not just an export.

**Procedure:** confirm current destination spec; export approved master; derive versions; verify filenames and manifest; watch each rendered file; deliver through the agreed channel; archive source and approval record.

**Practice:** package a 16:9 master, 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, SRT captions, and manifest from one timeline. Quality means no version loses core meaning, legibility, or technical integrity.
