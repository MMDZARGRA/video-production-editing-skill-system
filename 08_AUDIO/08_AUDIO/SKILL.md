# Audio

**Purpose:** deliver intelligible dialogue and purposeful atmosphere. Bad sound breaks trust faster than imperfect pictures.

**Procedure:** choose mic placement for the voice; monitor with headphones; set safe levels; control noise and reflections; record room tone; log problems before leaving location.

**Practice:** record the same dialogue with lavalier, boom, and camera mic; compare clarity, noise, and editability. Quality means dialogue is understandable in normal listening conditions without aggressive repair.
