# Strategy

**Purpose:** turn a vague request into a focused viewer outcome. Define objective, audience context, single promise, content angle, CTA, constraints, and one success metric before creative work begins.

**Core decisions:** Who must change? What should they think/feel/do? Why should they care now? What format earns attention without breaking the promise?

**Practice:** write three one-page briefs for the same product—awareness, education, conversion—and explain what changes in hook, proof, and CTA. Quality means one person can make the right trade-offs without another meeting.
