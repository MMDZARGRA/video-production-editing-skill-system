# Storytelling

**Purpose:** organize information into change. A story needs a starting state, desire/question, friction, meaningful beats, and a payoff; an explainer still needs progression.

**Method:** identify the audience's question, map the beats, locate the emotional or intellectual turn, and test whether the end repays the opening promise.

**Practice:** beat-map a favorite 60–120 second video, then make a new version with one beat removed and explain the effect. Quality means the viewer always knows what matters now and why the next moment is worth watching.
