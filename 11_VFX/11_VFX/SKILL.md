# VFX

**Purpose:** extend or correct images while preserving visual credibility and continuity. Plan effects early because clean plates, tracking markers, lighting reference, and handles are production decisions.

**Procedure:** define the invisible viewer requirement; capture references/plates; track, roto, composite, match grain/motion/light; inspect at full resolution.

**Practice:** replace a simple screen and remove one unwanted object from a locked shot. Quality means edges, perspective, light, motion blur, and grain do not reveal the composite.
